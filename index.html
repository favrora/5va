Appliance advice & guides
01Is the first call free?+

Yes. 5VA does not charge for the initial conversation, and there is no obligation to book. An on-site visit, diagnosis, parts, and repair work have separate charges, discussed before you proceed. This is a US local number; your carrier’s usual call or SMS terms apply.

02Do you sell appliances or spare parts?+

No. We only repair household appliances. We do not sell new or used appliances or provide retail spare parts. A part needed for an agreed repair is discussed as part of that service.

03Do you repair commercial equipment?+

No. We do not repair restaurant equipment, laundromat machines, walk-in refrigeration, or other commercial appliances. Our focus is household kitchen and laundry appliances.

04Where can I request a visit?+

We visit homes in Philadelphia ZIPs 19115 and 19116, all of Montgomery County, PA, and selected PA and NJ ZIP codes listed on our service-area page. We work 7 AM–8 PM daily, Eastern Time. Call or text to arrange your visit; there is no customer drop-off location.

05What should I send by SMS?+

Send your appliance type, brand and model if available, a short description of the problem, and your ZIP code. A photo of the accessible model label or error display can help. Leave the appliance installed; do not remove panels or move heavy equipment.
