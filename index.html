Talk to 5VA
01Is the call really free?+

Yes. We don’t charge you to call and discuss your project, and there is no obligation to book. A service visit, diagnosis, materials, and repair work can have separate charges. We’ll discuss those before you decide to proceed.

02Do you handle more than appliance repairs?+

Yes. Alongside appliance repairs, 5VA helps with general home repairs, furniture assembly, mounting, drywall and painting, doors, and everyday maintenance. Tell us about the job so we can confirm the right scope and next steps.

03Which parts of Philadelphia do you serve?+

Our focus is Philadelphia and the surrounding Pennsylvania communities, with a base in Feasterville-Trevose. Share your ZIP code when you call so we can confirm availability for your address and type of work.

04Can I get a price before the work starts?+

Describe the job and share any helpful photos. Some tasks can be discussed in advance; appliance faults and hidden problems may need an on-site assessment. Ask about the visit fee and the scope of the estimate before booking. Work proceeds after you agree to it.

05What should I have ready when I call?+

Your ZIP code, a short description of the problem, and your preferred time are a great start. For appliance repair, a brand and model number help. For home projects, photos and approximate dimensions make the conversation easier.
