Your questions, answered

01Can you adjust a cabinet door instead of replacing it?+

An adjustment may be appropriate, depending on the hardware and condition. Share photos of the door and hinges so we can discuss it.

Tell us the result you want and show the existing hardware. Dimensions, finish, and compatibility should be confirmed before ordering.
