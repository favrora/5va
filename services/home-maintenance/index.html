One list, clear priorities

Group tasks by room, add photos, and mark what matters most. This makes it easier to discuss materials and decide which jobs belong in the visit.

Plan around the property

Tell us about access, stairs, parking, tenant arrangements, and any building restrictions. For rental homes, confirm who can authorize work and how the property can be accessed.

Keep track of the details

Review the task list and expected cost before starting. At the end, go through the completed work and note anything to plan for next time.
