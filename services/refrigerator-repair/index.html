Sound familiar?

  • Refrigerator compartment feels warmer than usual
  • Water collecting inside or beneath the appliance
  • Unusual buzzing, clicking, or rattling
  • Heavy frost or an ice maker that has stopped working
  • Door seals that do not seem to close properly

Start with the symptom

A warm refrigerator can have several causes. We begin with the appliance and its actual behavior, rather than assuming the most expensive part needs replacing.

Know the next step

After assessment, discuss the proposed work and expected cost before deciding how to proceed. Parts, model, access, and the underlying fault can affect the repair.

A little preparation helps

Keep the model information and any error message handy. Leave the appliance in place, clear an accessible path, and use official food-safety guidance if cooling has been interrupted.

Three things to send us

  1. Brand, model and approximate appliance age
  2. What has changed, error codes and when it started
  3. Whether the refrigerator, freezer or both are affected

Helpful photos: The model label, display and any visible water or ice buildup.
