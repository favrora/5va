Your questions, answered

01Is any water at the bottom normal?+

Some models retain a small amount in the sump. Water above the usual level, poor draining, or a new odor is worth describing when you call.

02Should I pull the dishwasher out first?+

No. Leave it installed and clear access to the kitchen. Moving it can disturb water connections or wiring.

03What if the problem started after a new installation?+

Mention that when scheduling and keep the installation paperwork available. Check whether the installer or manufacturer already provides relevant coverage.
