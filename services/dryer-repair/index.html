A practical place to start

Laundry should not take three cycles and a guessing game. If your dryer is leaving clothes damp or making a new noise, tell 5VA Appliance Repair what you are noticing. Share whether the appliance is gas or electric, the brand and model, and how long the problem has been happening. We will talk through the details and arrange the next step.

Sound familiar?

  • Drum turns but clothes stay damp
  • Dryer does not start or stops mid-cycle
  • New squeaking, thumping, or scraping
  • Drum will not turn
  • Unusual heat or a burning smell

Look at the complete symptom

Drying problems can involve the appliance or airflow. Describe drying time, heat, and any recent change to the setup so the assessment starts with useful information.

Discuss the proposed work

After the issue is assessed, review the repair approach and expected cost. A separate vent or building issue may require a different service.

Know when to stop

Stop using a dryer with a burning smell, smoke, or unusual heat. For a suspected gas leak, leave the area and contact emergency services or the gas utility from a safe location.

Three things to send us

  1. Brand, model and gas or electric type
  2. Whether it starts, turns and produces heat
  3. Any unusual noise, smell or displayed error

Helpful photos: The model label, controls and accessible vent connection.

Your questions, answered

01Is a dryer repair the same as vent cleaning?+

They are different tasks. Tell us about the installation and symptoms so we can confirm what is covered and whether another service is needed.

02What should I tell you about my dryer?+

Share the brand, model, and whether it is gas or electric. Describe any change in heat, drying time, or noise so we can plan the assessment.

03What should I check before calling?+

With the dryer off and cool, check the accessible lint screen according to the owner’s manual. Leave internal parts and duct disassembly to an appropriate professional.
